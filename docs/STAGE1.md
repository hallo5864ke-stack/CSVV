# Stage 1 report: vehicle physics and five cars

## Verification status, read this first

Stage 1 was developed without access to Roblox Studio. What has been run:

- the physics modules that ship in the game, executed headlessly against two
  rigid-body test rigs (`lune run tests/run`);
- the car builder, executed against an offline Roblox instance library
  (`lune run tests/builder`);
- strict Luau type-checking of every script against the Roblox API, plus lint.

What has **not** been run is the game itself. The Roblox-facing parts
(suspension raycasts, impulses on the chassis, seating, network ownership,
lights, wheel animation, HUD) have never executed in the engine. Stage 1 is not
finished until the checklist at the end of this report passes in Studio.

## Completed features

**Physics (all five cars)**

- Raycast suspension per wheel: spring, damper, bump stop, anti-roll bars.
  Weight transfer, body roll and pitch come from the rigid body itself.
- Engine with a torque curve, idle, rev limiter and engine braking; automatic
  gearbox (7 to 9 gears) with shift times, kick-down and reverse.
- FWD, RWD and AWD drivetrains with a torque split.
- Tyres: slip-angle lateral force with a peak and fall-off, load sensitivity,
  combined grip limited by a friction circle, rolling resistance.
- Surface grip: dry asphalt, wet asphalt, dirt, gravel, grass, sand, snow, ice.
- Brakes with front/rear bias and ABS; traction control; stability control
  that brakes the outer front wheel when the car starts to slide.
- Handbrake locks the rear wheels (sliding friction, much less lateral grip).
- Speed-sensitive steering with rate limiting and Ackermann geometry.
- Aerodynamic drag and downforce; electronic top-speed limiter.
- Fuel consumption from engine power; engine temperature model.
- Standing still holds on slopes (static friction), with no creep.

The names are fictional so the game does not use real trademarks. Each car has
its own dimensions, weight distribution, centre-of-mass height, engine,
gearing, tyres, suspension, steering and brake setup.

**Lights and controls**

- Low and high beam, tail lights, brake lights with a high-level lamp,
  reversing lights, left/right indicators (front, mirror, rear) at 90 flashes
  per minute, hazard lights, self-cancelling indicators.
- Keyboard and gamepad driving controls, entering and leaving, recovery.
- Light state is server-authoritative and visible to all players.

**Multiplayer foundation**

- The driver's client simulates its car; the server simulates parked cars.
- The server validates every client message (sender must be the driver, types
  and ranges checked, rate-limited) and resets cars beyond their speed envelope.

Also checked for every car, all passing:

- reverse engages from standstill, is limited, and drive re-engages;
- no creep on the flat; handbrake holds a 15 % hill; the car rolls without it;
- handbrake alone stops much later than the foot brake;
- a sideways nudge at rest dies out (no low-speed jitter);
- full steering at 80 km/h does not spin the car; yaw settles on release;
- a full-lock lane change at 130 km/h stays under 12 degrees of sideslip;
- handbrake with steering breaks the rear loose;
- steering lock shrinks with speed; inner wheel steers more than the outer;
- combined tyre force never leaves the friction circle (20 000 random cases);
- all five cars differ pairwise in mass, acceleration, top speed, braking
  distance and cornering grip.

`lune run tests/builder`: each generated model matches its configured length,
width, height, ground clearance, wheelbase, track and front weight share; the
chassis mass equals the configured mass; tyres touch the ground and sit inside
the body; all lamp groups exist on the correct side and end; the driver's seat
is on the left with headroom; nothing is anchored and only the chassis has mass.

The two rigs are simplified (straight line; flat planar motion with
quasi-static load transfer). They exercise the real force code but not the
3-D suspension dynamics, which only Roblox's physics can show.

## Known limitations and open risks

- **Not playtested in Roblox Studio** (see above). Most likely places for a
  first-run fault: suspension settling at spawn, the hand-over of physics
  between server and driver when entering or leaving, and wheel animation.
- Car bodies are assembled from about 115 primitive parts each. They are
  complete, proportioned cars, but not sculpted meshes. Showroom-quality bodies
  need mesh models made in a 3-D tool; any model that keeps the part names
  listed in `VehicleBuilder` will work with the existing systems.
- Wheels are visual; tyre contact is one ray per wheel, so kerbs are climbed
  rather than struck by the tyre sidewall.
- Wheel spin is not simulated as a separate state: burnouts do not exist, and
  traction control and ABS are always on.
- Holding S at standstill selects reverse, so on a hill the foot brake holds
  for a moment and then the car reverses. Space (handbrake) is the hill hold.
- Other players' cars show static suspension height (steering and wheel spin
  are shown).
- Interiors are seats and a dashboard block only; instruments, the steering
  wheel, mirrors and the V/C cameras are stage 2.
- Engine, tyre and road sounds are not implemented yet. Roblox audio needs
  uploaded or licensed sound assets, which cannot be created from code.
- Touch controls are not implemented yet (stage 6); on mobile the jump button
  will eject the driver.
- The speed-envelope check limits speed cheating only; a full anti-teleport
  check belongs with `TrafficRulesSystem`.

## Studio checklist (required to close stage 1)

Open `dist/RussianDrivingSimulator.rbxlx`, press Play, and for **each** of the
five cars:

1. The car settles level on its wheels at spawn and stays still.
2. Hold F at the driver's door: you are seated and the HUD appears.
3. W accelerates through the gears; the speed and rpm readouts follow.
   Compare 0-100 km/h and top speed with the table above (the straight has a
   board every 100 m).
4. S brakes; the HUD shows the braking distance. Compare 100-0 km/h with the
   table, then repeat on the wet and dirt strips.
5. Hold S at standstill: reverse engages, reversing lights come on.
6. A/D steer; the front wheels turn and the car follows without twitching at
   speed. Drive the slalom.
7. Space at 60 km/h with steering: the rear slides. Space on the 15 % hill:
   the car holds.
8. Q, E, H, L: indicators blink and self-cancel after a turn, hazards blink,
   headlights cycle low/high; brake lights follow S.
9. Speed bumps: the suspension absorbs them and the wheels move.
10. F leaves the car; it stays parked. R rights an overturned car.

Then start a local server with two players (Test tab, Clients and Servers)
and confirm that the second player sees the first car move, steer and signal.

Please report anything that differs, with the car name and step number.
